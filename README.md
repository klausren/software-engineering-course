A complete, bilingual (English-primary with Chinese annotations) open course for **Software Engineering I** — a 16-week, 32-hour undergraduate course for international students, taught with a project-driven approach built around **HanPal**, a small programme that helps non-native speakers learn Chinese.

面向国际学生的《软件工程 I》开源课程：16 周 / 32 学时，英文主讲、中文注释，以 **HanPal**（帮外国人学习中文的小程序）贯穿全学期。

> 🆕 **2026-09-14 · Project change 项目更换**：The semester project is now **HanPal** — a WeChat mini programme that helps international students learn Chinese. It is student-owned from week 4: the team defines the users, the requirements and the priority order. The instructor is the sole reviewer.
> 学期大作业已更换为 **HanPal**（帮外国人学习中文的小程序）。从第 4 周起由学生自己定义用户、需求与优先级，任课教师是唯一评审人。
>
> **Two cases, two roles 两类案例、两种用途**：**CampusGuard**（smart campus access &amp; safety）is an **original case released under CC BY 4.0** and stays in this repository as the **classroom modelling case** — the exercises in the decks are written against it. **HanPal** is the **student team project** and is not distributed here, because it belongs to the teams that built it. Neither is derived from *SafeHome*, the Pressman &amp; Maxim case, which is not ours to relicense.
> CampusGuard（校园智能通行与安防）作为 **CC BY 4.0 原创课堂建模案例**保留在本仓库，课件中的建模练习都基于它；HanPal 是**学生团队大作业**，权属归各组，故不在此分发。两者都**不是** Pressman &amp; Maxim 的 SafeHome 的衍生品。

**Highlights 教学特色**

- 🔧 **Project-driven 项目驱动** — One semester-long team project (**HanPal**); every modeling technique learned in class is immediately applied to the team's own requirements. 每周所学建模技术即刻应用于团队自己的需求。
- 🌏 **Bilingual 双语** — English-primary slides with concise Chinese annotations to help international students master technical terms. 英文主讲，中文注释辅助术语理解。
- ✍️ **Hands-on 每课练习** — Each week includes 2–3 in-class exercises with answers. 每周 2–3 个课堂练习（含答案）。
- 🎨 **Original figures 原创图表** — All diagrams are original vector drawings (no copyrighted textbook images), so the materials are safe to remix. 全部图表为原创矢量图，不含教材原图。

## 🔐 Course Project 课程大作业：HanPal

**HanPal** is a WeChat mini programme that helps non-native speakers learn Chinese — survival scenarios, short daily practice, pronunciation feedback. Students work in **5 teams of 5–6** and own the project from week 4: the team defines the users, the requirements and the priority order, and defends them in a live review.

**HanPal** 是一个帮外国人学习中文的微信小程序（生存场景、每日短练习、发音反馈）。**5 组、每组 5–6 人**，从第 4 周起项目归团队所有：由团队自己定义用户、需求与优先级，并在现场评审中为之答辩。

**One topic, one rubric, one reviewer 一个题目、一套标准、一位评审人** — every team builds the same product against the same rubric, and the instructor is the sole reviewer. There is no client track and no instructor-supplied requirement track: the questions come from real users your team interviews, not from a brief you were handed.

**每组做同一个产品、用同一套评分标准、由任课教师唯一评审**。不存在甲方轨道、不存在教师给定需求轨道——需求来自你们自己访谈到的真实用户，不是发到手里的需求说明书。

| Item 项目 | Detail 说明 |
|---|---|
| Teams 分组 | 5 teams of 5–6 · 每组设组长 1 名 |
| Roles 角色 | Requirements · Analysis · Design · Build · Growth · Documentation（每人至少独占 1 个） |
| Milestones 里程碑 | M0 kickoff (W04) · M1 requirements (W05–W07) · M2 analysis · M3 design · M4 build · M5 growth · M6 final |
| Hard criteria 硬指标 | ≥8 real user interviews（不得全在本组内）· ≥2 personas · ≥8 use cases · requirements verifiable |
| Evidence 取证 | Every claim traces to an interview record — a requirement points at a quote, a quote points at a person. 每条需求都能回溯到访谈原话，原话能回溯到受访者 |
| Review 评审 | Stage reviews in class (W05–W15) → final defence (W16), individual statements from every member |

**The classroom modelling case 课堂建模案例** — the exercises inside the decks run on **CampusGuard**, the CC BY 4.0 case in [`03-project/CampusGuard-Project-Brief.md`](03-project/CampusGuard-Project-Brief.md): 20 functional requirements, 8 non-functional requirements, 5 core scenarios, and a client who withholds three constraints on purpose. Learn the technique on CampusGuard; build your own product in HanPal.
**课件里的建模练习基于 CampusGuard**（见上，CC BY 4.0）：20 条功能需求、8 条非功能需求、5 个核心场景，以及一位"故意隐瞒三项约束"的需求方。**在 CampusGuard 上练技术，在 HanPal 上做自己的产品。**
