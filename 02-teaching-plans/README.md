This directory will contain the **sanitized (public) versions** of weekly teaching plans and the course standard, released progressively alongside the weekly slides.

本目录将随课件逐周发布**脱敏公开版**教案与课程标准。

- Weekly teaching plans (W00–W16) and the course standard are fully prepared in the internal course package. 教案（W00–W16）与课程标准已在课程内部资料中完成。
- Public release is **pending review** of the following items to avoid leaking internal assessment details: 以下内容待确认脱敏后发布，避免泄露内部考核信息：

  - Internal course codes 校内课程编号（已替换为通用编号）
