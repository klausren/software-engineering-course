# SafeHome Project 项目说明

A semester-long team project for **Software Engineering I**. Students build a home-security system, **SafeHome**, applying each modeling technique learned in class — from requirements elicitation to architectural design.

《软件工程 I》贯穿全学期的团队项目：学生以 SafeHome（智能家居安全系统）为对象，将课堂所学从需求获取到架构设计的各项建模技术付诸实践。

---

## 1. Project Background 项目背景

SafeHome is a home-security system that lets homeowners monitor and control their residence remotely: sensors and video cameras detect events (intrusion, fire, flooding), and the owner is alerted via phone/PC and can respond.

SafeHome 是一款智能家居安全系统：通过传感器与摄像头监测入侵、火灾、漏水等事件，房主可通过手机/电脑远程查看并处置。

The project follows the classic SafeHome case study (adapted from R. S. Pressman, *Software Engineering: A Practitioner's Approach*) and is **original teaching material** — all diagrams in this repo are self-drawn. 项目取材自 Pressman 教材经典案例，仓库内全部图表为原创绘制。

## 2. Team Organization 团队组织

- The class (29 students) is divided into **5 teams of 5–6 members**. 全班 29 人分为 5 组，每组 5–6 人。
- Teams are formed **in class in Week 4** (project kickoff); each team elects a Project Manager and assigns roles. 第 4 周项目启动时现场分组，确定项目经理与分工。
- Each team maintains a version-controlled repository (GitHub / Gitee) and a task board. 每组建立版本仓库与任务看板。

## 3. Two-Track Requirements & Review 双轨需求与评审机制

To train different professional skills, the five teams follow **two tracks**:

| Track 轨道 | Teams 组数 | Requirements 需求来源 | Review model 评审方式 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Track A — Client-driven 甲方制** | 3 teams | **Client A** acts as the client and provides the requirements 甲方 Client A 提出需求 | **Dual review 共同评审**：Client A as domain expert + instructor as technical expert 甲方（领域专家）与教师（技术专家）共同评审 |
| **Track B — Instructor-driven 教师制** | 2 teams | The instructor provides the requirements 教师提供需求 | **Sole review 唯一评审**：by the instructor 教师作为唯一评审 |

**Rationale 教学意义**：Track A trains real-client communication, requirements negotiation, and multi-perspective trade-offs; Track B focuses on engineering correctness. The two modes complement each other. Track A 训练与真实客户沟通与多视角权衡，Track B 聚焦工程技术正确性，两种模式互补。

## 4. Milestones & Review Points 里程碑与评审时点

| Phase 阶段 | Content 内容 | Review 评审 |
|---|---|---|
| Practice I（Week 4–5） | Requirements inception & elicitation 需求启始与收集 | Requirements review 需求评审 |
| Modeling（Week 6–14） | Business process → use case → domain model → behavior → class modeling 业务流程 → 用例 → 领域 → 行为 → 类建模 | In-class phase reviews 各阶段随堂评审 |
| Final（Week 16） | Acceptance & defense 期末验收与答辩 | Final demo & defense 成果演示与答辩 |

## 5. Deliverables 交付物

Each team submits the project wiki with all artifacts by the end of the semester: 每组期末提交项目 wiki，含全部制品：

- Requirements specification 需求规格（用例图与用例描述）
- Analysis & design models 分析与设计模型（领域模型、行为模型、类模型）
- UI design 界面设计
- Architecture & final demo 架构设计与最终演示
